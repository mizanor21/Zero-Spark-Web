# Homepage

Scope: `/` (and the `/apply` stub it links to). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: university students and fresh graduates deciding whether to apply.
Job: understand what the one NextSpark program contains, week by week, and apply.
Primary action: "Apply to the program", leading to `/apply`.
Proof on the page: the owner's real curriculum only (16 weeks, 3 industry sessions, capstone, evaluation model, prerequisites).
Constraints: no invented partners, mentors, outcomes, fees or dates; brand colours and stack are fixed by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The program is one machined object. The page takes it apart in the order the program runs, names each part, and closes it again. It refuses the category page: stock-photo hero, equal course cards, counter stats, testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal #1C1C1C, bone #EDE8DF and barber red #B3261E used as page-scale grounds, not accents. An orthographic exploded-view object in charcoal metal, bone ceramic, one glass plate and a red core. Archivo expanded for headlines and condensed for labels, STIX Two Text for standfirsts. Hairline rules, 2px corners, a red leader line from the page edge to every section heading. Glass only on the nav, the mobile caption panel and the mobile menu.

STORY: The visitor understands this is one 16-week program that ends in a deployed capstone, believes it because every week, assignment and deliverable is shown, and applies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Charcoal ground. Left six columns: four-line display headline, serif standfirst, red "Apply to the program" button with a text link beside it, three facts under a hairline. Right 58%: the assembled object, large, closing shut on load. A glass nav bar floats at the top.

FORM: Exploded-view technical assembly, candidate 4 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:93afcabfad2d41b2. Signature interaction: scroll separates the plates along one axis; each part slides out to meet its label on a red leader line; a seven-stop rail scrubs the same axis. Reduced motion and short viewports get a static layout with an isometric line drawing.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
