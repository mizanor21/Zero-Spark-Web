# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: university students and fresh graduates who need industry-grade, job-ready IT skills and an industrial training credential. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12.

No secondary audience is confirmed. Working professionals and corporate/university buyers were offered and not chosen as the homepage's target.

## Product Purpose

NextSpark is an IT industrial training and professional development platform. The homepage succeeds when a student or fresh graduate applies to a program.

## Positioning

Undecided. The owner's stated ambition is that the brand reads as a premium international technology brand, not a generic educational website. The specific mechanism a neighbouring training provider could not truthfully claim has not been stated yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: apply to a program (admissions-led).
- Stack is fixed by the owner: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Motion, React Three Fiber.
- Undecided: where "Apply" leads (form, external link, or a later route), whether programs run as dated cohorts, and whether an account/login area exists.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NextSpark (inferred from the repository name; the owner did not correct it when asked).
- Brand colours, binding: Charcoal Black `#1C1C1C`, Bone White `#EDE8DF`, Barber Red `#B3261E`.
- Binding direction from the owner: premium futuristic technology aesthetic, editorial-inspired typography, sophisticated 3D visual elements, selective glassmorphism, smooth scroll-based animation, immersive hero, asymmetric layouts, premium navigation and footer, excellent responsive behaviour.

## Evidence on Hand

- The owner has a real program list and curriculum. It has not been supplied to the repository yet.
- Not on hand, and never to be fabricated as real: instructor or mentor profiles, hiring-partner or university logos, placement numbers, alumni stories, testimonials, a logo or wordmark file, photography.

## Product Principles

1. The applicant decides. Every section either helps a student judge whether a program is right for them or gets out of the way of applying.
2. Show the work, not the promise. Curriculum, projects, and what a trainee actually builds carry the page; outcome claims appear only when real.
3. Read as a technology company that trains people, not a school that teaches technology.
4. Nothing invented stands in for evidence. Placeholder material is labelled until the real thing replaces it.
